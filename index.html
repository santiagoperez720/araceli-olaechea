    (About)

    Writing on the cultural and behavioral dimensions of fashion.

    Araceli Olaechea Landa is a fashion journalist and writer with experience at V Magazine, CR Fashion Book, and COSAS. She holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from the Pontifical Catholic University of Peru, an academic foundation that shapes her interest in the cultural and behavioral dimensions of fashion. Her professional background spans editorial writing, public relations, and creative direction, supported by proficiency in digital strategy and design. Raised bilingual in Spanish and English within an international environment, she aims to produce thoughtful content that connects with diverse audiences.
